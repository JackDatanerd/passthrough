REGRESSION FOUND AND FIXED FIRST
- The Scan/ATS push (2124535) shipped a scan.controller.js that dropped `version: scan.docTemplateVersion` (and the row.* form) from ALL SEVEN
  getDesignTokens call sites that 4da7fee had added. Effect in production: look versioning was inert - every document, including those of scans
  stamped look 2, was drawn on look 1 again. Restored at all seven sites. tests/docTemplate.test.js already guards this; it failed on the fresh clone.
  (That session worked from a copy of scan.controller.js older than 4da7fee. Anything else it touched in that file should be re-checked the same way.)

WHAT WAS WRONG WITH THE LOOK
Rendered and looked at: tidy and ATS-safe but not covetable. The credential (what the customer pays for) was a tiny grey line; the four
presets differed only in colour; role, employer and city ran together in one bold line; the left-edge stripe read as a glitch; a short resume left the
bottom third bare.

WHAT CHANGED (look 3, src/services/pdfSignature.service.js; looks 1 and 2 untouched)
1. Credential seal in the header: outlined (tinted for Creative) mark with a drawn check, "PASSTHROUGH VERIFIED" and the verify address printed in it. A
   Scan Report (not Verified) gets a plain grey seal and honest wording. No seal when there is no link.
2. Role (bold) / employer (ink colour, semibold) · location (muted) / dates (right, tabular figures). DOM order stays role, employer, dates.
3. Four different designs on one skeleton:
     Traditional  centred letterhead, double rule, serif small caps-style headings with a hairline
     Professional left letterhead, hairline, sans headings with a short accent bar
     Modern       top rule, square-marker headings running into a rule
     Creative     large serif name with accent bar, tinted pill seal, serif title-case headings, italic employer
4. Spacing follows the page: up to +30% room for a short resume (docFit.airinessFor), tightened when just over a page (look-3 capacity calibrated by
   rendering 96 resumes at both paper sizes: capacity 48 A4 / 44.9 Letter, squeeze 12%).
5. Symmetric margins; the edge stripe is gone; bullets and dates use the ink; cover letter uses the same letterhead.
6. ATS safety is by construction: one column, no tables, images or scripts, real text, parser order name -> contact -> credential.
7. design.service.js: LATEST_LOOK 3 (same presets and the same ink a person already had); migration 0102 widens the check to 1..3 and defaults new scans to 3.

NOT CHANGED
- The Word file (ATS-first; its layout is deliberately plain). Looks 1 and 2. The credential wording, links and verification page.

CAVEAT
- Rendered and checked with WeasyPrint here, not the production browser renderer; spacing may differ slightly. Capacity numbers are set at the safe end.
