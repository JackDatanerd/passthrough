WEBHOOKS ROUND 24 (section 8, independent pass)

B1  A charge.dispute.* event that matched no payment was closed PROCESSED, so Replay answered 409 right after the owner alert said "repair the
    payment row, then Replay", and no sweep could pick it up (the sweep only looks at known payments). It now ends IGNORED with the note
    'payment not found', exactly like a refund with no payment. (webhooks.controller.js processDispute)
B2  The stored copy of an event (payload, key, type, reference) is now cleaned of U+0000 and lone surrogates. Postgres refuses both in jsonb /
    text, which answered a signed event 500 and made Paystack redeliver the same poison for ~72 hours. If the database still refuses a body,
    recordEvent records the event without it (the live delivery is processed from memory). (redactEvent, recordEvent, minimalResendEvent)
B3  The Resend "deliveries are being refused" notice compared a refusal with a health stamp the handler writes at most once per 10 minutes, so
    a delivery that got through could sit behind a newer refusal and the notice said "nothing has got through since". For Resend a refusal now
    counts once it is newer than the stamp by more than the stamp window; Paystack (exact, read from the inbox) is unchanged.
    (webhookRejections.js RESEND_STAMP_WINDOW_SECONDS / STAMP_LAG_MS, resend-webhook.controller.js)
B4  Delivery health compared payments.created_at (checkout start) with the webhook's received_at, and graced a sale from checkout. It uses
    paid_at (falls back to created_at; retries without the column on a database older than 0064). (computeWebhookHealth)
B5  The old-open-disputes sweep rotated through DISPUTED payments with last_reconciled_at - the marker sweepReversedPayments uses to skip a
    payment for a while - so the lost-dispute backstop skipped every dispute that sweep had just looked at. It now has its own
    payments.dispute_checked_at. (fetchOldOpenDisputes)
G1  refund.failed was an email and nothing else. It now stamps payments.refund_failed_at (SUCCESS / DISPUTED only); Admin -> Payments shows
    "Refund failed <date> - the buyer has not been paid back" on the row; the full refund that finally goes through clears it; a partial one
    does not. Also: mappers.paymentRowToCamel (refundFailedAt), the webhook self-test probes both new columns.
