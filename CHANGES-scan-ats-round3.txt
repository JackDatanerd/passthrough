BUGS FIXED
 B1  (HIGH) Keyword scoring was decided by the posting's opening paragraph: ties in frequency were broken by position, so "the
     top 18" was the title + company blurb and every skill listed lower down was cut. Probe: a well-matched senior-backend resume
     scored keywords 36 / total 73 (fail); deleting only "About <Company>" gave 68 / 84. Postings are now split into zones
     (company/benefits/EEO weighted down, requirements/responsibilities/title up) and skill-shaped terms (curated tool list,
     acronyms, CamelCase) are boosted. Works on flattened URL text too. Probe now: 68 / 84 either way.
 B2  (HIGH) "Try Again" after editing the delivered resume could never succeed: the owner's own figure/skill was judged against the
     ORIGINAL upload and flagged as invented on every attempt, and restoreFactualFields reverted their title/date corrections.
     New scans.user_edited_resume_data is the baseline for the retry's fabrication guard + factual restore.
 B3  Delivered-resume edit, cover-letter write and PDF regeneration are pinned to the delivery they were built from
     (resume_ats_path). Two concurrent saves could both win (losing the first hash from the history, orphaning its files); a
     cover letter / PDF built from the pre-edit resume could attach to the post-edit one.
 B5  updateDeliveredResume stored a resume_hash for FIX_PLAIN; generateFix stores null. Now consistent.
 B6  runAtsScan: a redelivery while the first run was still working started a second run (double Claude bill / e-mail). A SCANNING
     scan is taken over only when stale (120s); otherwise the queue consumer retries in 60s instead of acking. The catch block no
     longer flips an already-finished scan to ERROR or refunds its slot.
 B7  getScan no longer returns job lease / credit-round / error-recovery bookkeeping. initiateFix: bad JSON body -> 400.
 (B4 — score basis shifting on first edit of an uploaded file — is closed by G2 making the formatted-file score visible up front.)
