APPLICANT-FACING DOCUMENTS - round 4 (base: upstream d43a44d; findings of the fourth and fifth independent passes)
No migration, no new dependency.

Bugs fixed
- B1  GET /api/scan/:id/cover-letter-pdf was mounted on `pdfRegen`, the budget of the PAID regenerate-pdf route: eight letter
      downloads while tweaking a letter locked the buyer out of regenerating their resume PDF (the fate-sharing the draft PDF was
      just split out of). Own limiter, rl.coverLetterPdf (rl:coverletterpdf, 8 per 15 min).
- B2  A phone written "(+254) 712 345 678" was not linked in either format (the plus was only accepted at the very start). The plus
      now counts anywhere before the first digit and is kept in the tel: link; digit-count and shape guards unchanged.

Gaps closed
- G1  The Word files now look like the PDF's set. The DOCX (resume and cover letter) was always fixed navy while the PDF follows the
      user's palette (30 palettes). generateAtsDocx / generateCoverLetterDocx take `accent` (a 6-digit hex; anything else falls
      back to the template blue #1E40AF, which is also what the saved-profile PDF uses, so profile files match too). Every delivered
      DOCX site (updateDeliveredResume, generateFix candidates + final, generateBadge, free draft, cover letter) passes
      accentFor(scan) - the same design tokens the PDF is rendered from.
