Payments & Pricing — round 6 (independent audit; gap G1 + bugs B1–B6)

G1  FEATURE GAP — a buyer who paid twice for one resume was never told and was never refunded unless the
    owner noticed an alert. Now:
    - NEW src/services/refund.service.js: the queue-a-refund logic (per-payment claim, Paystack refund-list
      check that fails closed, open/processed guards, completing-leg maths) moved out of
      payments.controller.refundPayment so the admin button and the automatic path share it.
    - fulfillment.settlePayment: a DUPLICATE outcome is refunded in full automatically — only when the owning
      payment is proven (a different row, same scan), never for a free-credit row, idempotent across verify /
      recheck / webhook redelivery. Runs deferred behind the webhook; a deferred failure pages the owner.
    - AUTO_REFUND_DUPLICATES=false (wrangler.toml [vars]) switches it off.
    - verifyPayment answers { duplicate: true, refund: 'QUEUED' | 'REVIEW' }; PaymentSuccess shows a
      "you were charged twice" screen (reference shown, no auto-redirect).
    - owner alerts say "automatic refund queued (no action needed)" or "refund by hand — automatic refund
      failed (reason)"; the buyer's reversal email for a refunded duplicate says "extra payment refunded, your
      resume is unaffected" instead of "purchase closed".
B1  An admin-ACCEPTED underpayment left payments.amount_cents at the expected amount, so a later full refund
    of what was actually captured summed to less than amount_cents and the refund webhook judged it
    "partial" (sale never reversed, commission never clawed back). reconcile.recheckPayment now records the
    captured amount when it is LESS than expected (an overpayment keeps the expected price); the audit entry
    carries amountAdjusted.
B2  A mismatch found by the checkout guard, cancel guard or hourly sweep never alerted anyone (the sweep
    skipped it assuming the webhook had). notifySettlementProblem now alerts MISMATCH (not for the admin's
    own recheck); the sweep summary includes held items.
B3  Resuming a fresh pending checkout compared only tier + referral code, so a promo ending/starting or a
    partner tier price changing inside the 30-minute window charged the OLD amount. Resume now also requires
    pending.amount_cents === today's price; otherwise 409 "price changed" with the cancel reference.
B4  usePricing: a failed refetch at the promo deadline left the expired promo prices on screen beside a
    "showing standard pricing" notice, and failures were never retried. A lapsed promo entry is dropped on a
    failed refetch; failed fetches retry up to 4 times with a growing wait.
B5  Pricing page printed two stacked "Referral code applied" confirmations. ReferralCodeEntry owns it now; the
    page only adds a note when the code discounts SOME plans.
B6  PaymentSuccess: a 429 ended polling for good and the manual "Check again" restarted the whole cycle. It now
    retries itself after ~65s, at most twice.

Tests: tests/payments.round6.test.js (27), PaymentSuccess round 6 (4), usePricing.round6 (5); updated three
existing tests that encoded the old behaviour (DUPLICATE response shape, sweep mismatch silence, a resume
fixture priced below the real FIX price). Full suite 3288 passing; lint clean; wrangler dry-run bundles.
