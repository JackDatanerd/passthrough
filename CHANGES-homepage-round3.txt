Homepage — round 3: mobile responsiveness and the small remaining details. Frontend only.
Method: the built app was rendered in headless Chromium at 320/360/375/390/414/768/1024/1280px with the API mocked,
and measured (horizontal overflow, where the upload box lands, tap-target sizes, input font sizes, anchor offsets).
The original homepage (commit before our work) was built and measured the same way for comparison.

FOUND AND FIXED
1. Phone first screen (regression from round 2). The upload box sat at y=916 on a 390x800 phone (the original: 826),
   i.e. entirely below the fold, behind the pitch, proof row and facts. The hero is now three blocks in the order
   pitch -> scan card -> proof on phones (desktop layout unchanged: card top-aligned on the right, spanning both
   rows). Upload box now at y=544, with its mode tabs visible. Headline steps down to 34px on phones.
2. Horizontal scroll at 320px (page 334px wide). Cause: the receipt ticket's grid column could not shrink below its
   content. Columns are min-w-0, the score row uses minmax(0,1fr), the big score and paddings step down on phones.
   Verified: no overflow at any of the eight widths.
3. Navbar at 320-360px: "Sign in" and "Get started" wrapped to two lines and touched the logo. Pricing leaves the bar
   below sm, "Sign in" leaves it below 340px (both remain in "More"), items no longer wrap, and bar items get
   thumb-sized vertical hit areas.
4. iOS Safari zoomed the page on focus for every field smaller than 16px (ten of them, including the scan form's).
   Shared Input / Textarea / Select now use 16px text below sm (14px from sm up) and a 44px field on phones; the
   homepage's own inputs follow suit.
5. Tap targets: "Read my story" was 20px tall (now 44), the 7/30-day and Before/After toggles, the embed-format
   toggle, placement tabs, "Show all fields", footer links (20px -> 32px), the scan form's mode toggles and the
   employer submit button were all 28-36px. All raised.
6. Pricing on a phone listed Free, a locked Credential only, THEN the recommended Full fix. Full fix is now first on
   phones/tablets (desktop keeps it in the middle). Score-picker zone labels were clipped ("80+: CR...") and have a
   short form for narrow screens.
7. Count-up: the stats started at "0+ / 0%" and stayed there for anything that never scrolled them into view (a
   screenshot, a link preview, a failed animation frame). The real number is now the default; it restarts from 0
   only when the band is actually revealed (reset happens before paint, so no flash).
8. Sticky phone bar: now also hides while the closing call to action is on screen (it said the same thing, over it),
   and the page leaves room under the footer so the last footer row is never covered.
9. Employer form: the field <select> label was cut off ("(optiona"); shortened. Scan form email placeholder was
   clipped at the new 16px size; shortened to "Email me the result (optional)".
10. Phones show three pull-quotes instead of six (saves ~400px of an already long page; all six from sm up).
11. Small text: delta arrows 11->12px, Sample tags and ring caption 10->11px, the live dot respects reduced motion.

MEASURED, NOT CHANGED
- Anchor jumps land correctly: the navbar is static, not sticky, so nothing hides under it.
- 360x640 (small Android): the upload box starts at y=619, so only its top edge is in view.
- Page length on a phone: 15.7k px (the original: 6.7k). DOM nodes 873 (265). Main bundle +~17KB gzip since the
  pre-homepage baseline (part of that is other sessions' work). Home is not code-split.

NOT DONE ON PURPOSE
- viewport-fit=cover (iOS landscape notch): the page would then need safe-area padding everywhere.
- Shared Button is left at its current height; only the homepage's own submit buttons were raised.
