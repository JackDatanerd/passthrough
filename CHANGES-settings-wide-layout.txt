SETTINGS: USE THE WIDE SCREEN, AND FIX THE SAVED-PROFILE ROW
==============================================================

What changed for the person using it
- Settings was one narrow column (about 500px) on a wide screen. It now uses the dashboard's full width and is grouped:
    Account and security: two columns. Left: Account (name, email) and Email preferences. Right: Change password, Sign out
      other sessions, Signed-in devices. (One column on phones and narrow windows.)
    Saved profiles: the full width of the page.
    Your data: Your data and Scan history side by side, then the Danger zone across the page.
- Saved profile: the summary (saved date, name, latest role, counts, "View source scan") sits in its own panel, and the
  actions (Scan against a new job, Edit, Copy as another profile, Download .docx, Download PDF, Remove saved profile) are
  on a row beneath it that wraps. Before, the summary was squeezed into a thin column and the buttons ran out of the card.
- Other profiles: cards in a two-column grid, each with its actions wrapping beneath; the one being edited spans both
  columns so the editor has room.
- Cards use the same rounded corners as the rest of the dashboard (also the Signed-in devices card).

No text, behaviour, endpoint or data changed: only layout. Headings and labels are exactly as before.
