My Story: when a person is asked "did it lead to an interview?" — audit round 1. Migration 0120.
Base commit: 6473c79. Fresh-clone audit of the question's conditions (dashboard / My Story prompt, follow-up email sweep); 10 findings, all closed.

THE RULE, NOW IN ONE PLACE
 lib/outcomes.js evaluateAsk() is the single definition. The dashboard prompt and the email sweep pre-filter in SQL and re-check every row with it; the admin simulator (#10) calls it directly, so the explanation can not drift from the behaviour.
   dashboard   delivered 14..120 days ago (from the FIRST delivery), never answered; a "still applying" answer is asked again 30 days after it was given (up to 1 year); live purchase; rewrite did not fail.
   email       delivered 30..120 days ago, never answered, never mailed, < 3 attempts; owner active, verified, not deleted, opted in; live purchase; rewrite did not fail; owner not mailed in the last 30 days.

FINDINGS CLOSED
 1  Answers could never be changed in the UI. /pending now also returns `answers`; My Story shows "Your answers" with "Change my answer" (the same question, minus the current answer; warns that leaving "yes" takes a shared story down; My Story re-reads its stories afterwards).
 2  "Still applying" was a dead end. The dashboard asks again 30 days after (OUTCOME_REASK_AFTER_DAYS), up to a year after delivery; worded "You told us you were still applying. Any news?". Not emailed a second time.
 3  Email sweep starvation. The queue is read in pages (up to OUTCOME_SWEEP_MAX_PAGES) and each page is judged before the next; answered scans / ineligible people can no longer fill the window. Stable order (first_delivered_at, id).
 4  Refunded / disputed purchases are no longer asked (dashboard, email), can not submit an answer or story (409), and their stories are never shown publicly (toPublicStory; the admin story list flags them). Their earlier answers still count in the totals — dropping them would only remove the unhappy ones. Payment lookups fail open, like purchaseIsLive.
 5  One email per person: sending stamps their other in-window scans as asked, and nobody is mailed again within OUTCOME_EMAIL_USER_COOLDOWN_DAYS (30).
 6  Dashboard and email now share the 120-day limit (OUTCOME_ASK_MAX_AGE_DAYS; the old OUTCOME_EMAIL_MAX_AGE_DAYS is removed). The dashboard reads pages until three askable scans are found, so more than 25 answered scans no longer hide an unanswered one.
 7  Retries and owner edits rewrite fix_generated_at and used to restart both clocks. scans.first_delivered_at (migration 0120, stamped once by a trigger, backfilled) is what all timing uses now.
 8  A credential-only (BADGE) delivery is asked about "your credential", not a "fixed resume"; a delivery whose rewrite failed (the unchanged resume was handed back) is not asked at all.
 9  The follow-up email links to /dashboard/story (the tab that holds the question and the answers) instead of /dashboard#outcome, whose anchor could not exist before the card loaded.
 10 Simulator: GET /api/admin/outcome-ask/:scanId?days=N (admin only, read-only, no address returned) and a panel on Admin > Prompt outcomes: "asked now? by dashboard / by email? why? what changes after N more days?".

ROUND 2 (audit pass 2 findings A–E; base 1fbf7a3/c20e1e1 — upstream took migration 0121 for itself, so this is 0122)
 A  The outcome clock starts at the first REAL delivery. Migration 0122 reworks the 0120 trigger: a delivery whose rewrite FAILED does not stamp scans.first_delivered_at (the successful retry does); moving a scan BADGE -> FIX clears it (the rewrite is what is delivered next); rolling that back to BADGE restores it; an UPDATE that merely names fix_generated_at again never stamps. Backfill clears the stamp on scans whose current delivery is a failed one. evaluateAsk no longer falls back to fix_generated_at: no stamp = not delivered.
 B  "Report an interview" at any time: /pending returns `reportable` (delivered, live, unanswered fixes that are not being asked about right now — too early or past the limit; up to 10). My Story lists them under "Got an interview already?" with only a "yes" option (a "not yet" on day 3 would be meaningless and would switch the real question off).
 C  "Not yet" is asked once more on the dashboard 30 days after it was given (OUTCOME_NOT_YET_REASK_AFTER_DAYS), within 120 days of delivery, and only if that answer was never revisited (updated_at still equals its first answered_at). Worded "You told us “not yet”. Any news?". Not emailed.
 D  Interview details of an INTERVIEW answer are kept when the person taps "yes" again (it used to wipe them). Once a story is APPROVED, changing the two numbers it shows without the story is refused (409); changing them together with the story sends it back to review even when its words are unchanged.
 E  /pending marks `locked` on an answer whose purchase was refunded or disputed; My Story shows why instead of a button that would fail.
