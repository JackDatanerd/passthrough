# Pilot operating checklist mapped to the screens that now exist

The procedure is `docs/experts/phase0/03-pilot-runbook.docx`. The runbook was written for a fully manual pilot; this maps each step to what the app now does. Where a step still needs you, it says so. Screens: **Admin > Human orders** (`/admin/human`: Orders, Experts, Summary), **Expert site** (`/expert`), **Customer** (`/human/order`, `/human/orders`).

| Runbook step | Now | Screen |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Before the first order: experts signed, addendum and wording live, style guide shared, recording sheet set, cap and tiers set | Experts are created and switched on only after you mark their agreement signed (the app will not assign to anyone else). Cap = 15 paid orders (settings). Tiers: 7-day, 48-hour, 24-hour on; polish off. Everything else (counsel, style guide, bars) is still yours. | Experts tab |
| 1. Shared edit tool for redacted copies | **Not needed**: the expert site is the redacted editing tool. | Expert site |
| Step 1 Intake: record job id, tier, cover letter, region, paid time; deadline clock starts when paid with a complete brief | Done by the app: job number, tier, cover letter, region and paid time are on the row; promised and internal deadlines are computed from the paid time (24, 48, 168 hours; buffers 2, 4, 12). The brief form will not submit without a target role and the three ticks. | Orders list, order panel |
| Step 1: brief incomplete / pending expiry | The brief cannot be sent incomplete. An unpaid order is closed after 24 hours by the hourly tick. | Orders (filter: awaiting payment) |
| Step 2 Assignment | Choose an expert in the order panel; only experts who are on, have two-factor and a signed agreement appear; at most two open jobs each. The expert sees the job in their list at once. **You still message them and ask for a yes**; if they cannot, **Take it back from the expert** and assign another. | Order panel: Assign / Take it back |
| Step 2: pay is the standard amount from the first job | Pay is stored on the job at checkout. | Order panel |
| Step 3 Redaction | **Automatic**: contact details, links, referee blocks and the customer's name are replaced by markers before the expert ever sees the resume, and the server refuses any edit that touches them. Supporting files are masked the same way. The expert never receives the original file. | n/a |
| Step 4 Expert work, questions, active minutes | The expert edits in the workbench and may ask up to three questions; the customer is emailed; the deadline clock pauses until the answer (or 24 hours). Active minutes are counted while they type (idle time is not). Change summary and confirmation items: **you** write the summary and tick the confirmations at delivery (the expert has no field for them in Phase 1). | Expert site; order panel (minutes, questions) |
| Step 5 Re-scan and fact check | **Automatic and shown to you**: the restored resume, the ATS score before and after (the same scorer as the scan flow), locked details present or missing, and any figure that is in nothing the customer gave. Delivery is blocked on a missing locked detail or an unconfirmed figure. Read the whole document once as the client would. The Word and PDF files are built by the app. | Order panel: Review and deliver |
| Step 5: send back with the failing lines | **Send back to the expert** (a note is required). | Order panel |
| Step 6 Delivery | **Deliver to the customer** builds and stores the files and emails the customer. The email states the 72-hour acceptance rule; the order page shows the change summary, scores, figures to check, and the revision button. | Order panel; customer order page |
| Step 7 Acceptance, revision, rating | Customer: Accept, or one revision (7 days; 24- or 48-hour clock; same expert), then a 1 to 5 rating. Silence for 72 hours is accepted by the hourly tick. You can **Mark accepted** if the customer says yes by email. A changed target role is a new order: **you** handle that by hand. A second revision is your call outside the app. | Customer order page; order panel |
| Step 8 Lateness, refund, credit | Overdue jobs are flagged in the list. **Refund** uses the existing refund path (the job closes and earnings reverse when Paystack confirms). **Issuing a rush-premium credit is NOT built**: record the amount you gave in "Late credit given" so the margin is right, and give it by hand outside the app. | Orders list; order panel: Refund, Record |
| Step 9 Manual payout and ledger | Pay is written once at acceptance with the 3-day hold (setting). Experts tab shows available / on hold / paid per expert. Pay them yourself, then enter the payment reference and **Record payout**; the minimum is $25 and a repeat with the same reference changes nothing. Tax and identity checks before the first payout are still yours. | Experts tab |
| Recording sheet fields | Active minutes (automatic, or override), questions, revision, time to delivery, score before and after, rating, cost (expert pay plus "Other cost"), late credit. | Order panel; Summary tab |
| 4. Weekly review and the Gate tab | **Summary tab** gives paid, delivered, accepted, refunded, on-time %, revision %, average minutes, average rating, average score gain, revenue, cost and margin. The gate thresholds are still in `12-phase0-gate-sheet.xlsx`. | Summary tab |
| Stop the pilot at once (privacy incident, invented fact reaches a client) | **Switch every expert off** (Experts tab) or set `EXPERTS_ENABLED=false` (every route 404s; do not do this with open orders: refunds will no longer close their jobs). | Experts tab |
