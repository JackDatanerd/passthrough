Result screen, round 4: "Where your points went" (no migration)
Base: origin/main b57fa15 (rounds 1-3 applied). The offer card, tiers and pricing are untouched.

WHAT CHANGED
- New card between the score card and "Why this score": the three biggest places the score loses points, worst first, each with the
  plain reason ("−17 Keyword match: Missing from your resume: kubernetes, terraform").
  Header: "48 of 100 points are missing, and 28 of them stand between you and the 80."
- Backend: ats.service exports CATEGORY_WEIGHTS (0.35 / 0.25 / 0.20 / 0.20, the weights scoreResume already uses) and buildAtsDetail
  returns them as atsDetail.weights. A job description with nothing scoreable (keywords.noKeywords) drops the keyword weight to 0,
  matching scoreResume's own re-weighting. The existing atsDetail.keywords shape is unchanged.
- Frontend: lib/pointsLost.js splits the gap between the score and 100 across the categories in proportion to weight x shortfall,
  with largest-remainder rounding, so the points shown always add up to the real gap exactly, whatever the AI blend did to the final
  score. The weights come from the server, so the page carries no second copy that could drift.

HONESTY RULES
- Hidden when the server sent no weights (older API), there is no score, or no category has a shortfall to attribute.
- The wording says the points are "split by how much each part counts": an attribution of the real gap, not a claim that fixing one
  category recovers exactly that many points.
