APPLICANT-FACING DOCUMENTS - round 8 (base: upstream 29e59ad; findings of the ninth pass, with the owner's Word screenshots)
No migration, no new dependency.

What the Word screenshots confirmed (real Word, not a stand-in): no "[Compatibility Mode]"; Georgia name and headings with the rules;
tinted body colour; two-row contact line with the credential link below; dates flush right; hanging bullets; the Arabic bullet's marker on
the right and English bullets untouched; "Name · Page 2 of 2" on page 2 only; the letterhead, rule and date of the letter; and
"Accessibility: Good to go".

Gaps closed
- G1  Word marked the applicant's own details as spelling errors (red squiggles under the LinkedIn handle, the portfolio and project URLs,
      "Optimiser", "Honours", the name in the footer). Two causes, both fixed in docx.service.js:
      - no document language: Word judged British spelling by its US default. The language now follows the paper (A4 -> en-GB, Letter ->
        en-US), in the resume and the letter.
      - nothing exempted proper nouns and jargon: `noProof` is now set on the name, the contact and credential lines (text and links), the
        footer, job/degree/project/role title lines and their dates or tech lists, skills, languages, certifications, awards and the project
        link. The prose - summary, bullets, education details, project descriptions, the letter body - is still checked, so the
        applicant keeps real typo help where it matters.
- G2  Letters outside basic Latin fell back to a system font in the middle of a word (Ł, ż, ś, ę, ř, ğ, ș, ễ ... rendered in a different
      face from their neighbours - seen in real Chromium with Polish, Czech, Turkish, Romanian and Vietnamese text). The embedded fonts now
      carry the Latin Extended and Vietnamese subsets as well (nine faces, each with its own unicode-range read from the package CSS so
      the browser picks the right file per character): ~70 KB -> ~178 KB raw. Re-verified in Chromium: those names render in Inter
      throughout, and the fit calibration of the 56-size grid is unchanged.
