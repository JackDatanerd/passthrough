Section 8 (Webhooks) — round 11 (fresh independent audit of webhooks.controller.js, webhooks.routes.js, resend-webhook, reconcile sweeps)
Base: a44baa3 "Scan/ATS round 5 ..."

NO MIGRATION. Nothing to apply before deploying.

GAPS
G1  A failed automatic refund was never retried. refund.service tells the owner "the next verify / webhook redelivery also
    retries it", but nothing re-ran it once Paystack stopped redelivering (about 72 h) and the buyer never returned: a
    duplicate or undeliverable payment stayed charged for nothing. New hourly sweepFailedAutoRefunds (reconcile.service,
    called from webhookMaintenanceSweep): finds SUCCESS payments 20 min - 14 days old that bought nothing (no scan, scan
    missing, another payment owns the scan, or unclaimed scan of a deleted account) and re-runs the same autoRefund
    path. Budget 5 attempts per payment per week (an IN_PROGRESS refund is not an attempt and gives the slot back),
    10 payments per run; the owner gets one summary of what was re-queued and one page, once a week per payment, for
    anything it gave up on. The misleading text in fulfillment.service and the refund.failed alert now says what really
    happens.
G2  An inbox event that can never succeed (FAILED forever, HELD for a payment that was settled by hand, RECEIVED row for a
    Worker that died) could only be left to keep alerting and being re-driven. New POST /admin/webhook-events/:id/dismiss
    (FAILED / HELD / RECEIVED only, 409 otherwise, guarded update, note up to 200 chars, audit-logged as webhook.dismiss)
    closes it as IGNORED and keeps it visible with the admin's note. Admin → Webhooks gets a Dismiss button + confirmation.
G3  The delivery-health alert and /healthz stayed red for up to 7 days after webhooks were fixed: one old sale with no
    charge.success counted until it aged out of the window. Health now also reports `unrecovered` = missing sales newer
    than the latest charge.success received for this app's payments; the alert and the banner use that.
G4  A partially accepted dispute found by the dispute sweep was skipped silently, and the sweep read only the first 100
    disputes. Partial ones now page the owner once a week per dispute; the list is paged (100 x 5).
G5  The webhook inbox had no date filter. from / to (YYYY-MM-DD, inclusive) on the API and two date fields on the page.

BUGS
B1  The dispute sweep re-ran a charge.dispute.create it (or a live delivery) had already acted on. If an admin cleared the
    dispute meanwhile and Paystack still listed it open, the payment went straight back to DISPUTED. It now skips a create
    whose inbox row is PROCESSED with a "marked DISPUTED" note.
B2  The webhook_events prune ignored its own error, so a failing prune was invisible. It is logged now.
B3  The stale-event re-drive counted any returned outcome as "recovered", including HELD. Only PROCESSED counts.
B4  closeResolvedHeldEvents only looked at the oldest 100 HELD rows, so 100 genuinely waiting rows hid every later,
    resolved one. It pages with a received_at cursor (5 x 100).
(Frontend) the date inputs on Admin → Webhooks accept only real calendar days (2026-99-99 is ignored).
