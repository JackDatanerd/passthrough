    human_forty_eight_hour | human_twenty_four_hour), scan_id NULL, prices read from the settings row, never hard-coded.

    The verify call, the webhook, the sweeps and an admin recheck all land on it; every repeat changes nothing.

Codes

    The order code imports none of that code; a test proves it with five code kinds and the promo window active.

    story, notes, up to three top results, up to 3 files (Word / text PDF, 5 MB) and 5 links. The 18+ tick, third-party rights tick
    and optional improvement consent (6.2) use the wording of docs/experts/phase0/06-checkout-wording (see DECISIONS 5).
Admin inbox (existing admin area, same auth, step-up on money and privacy routes)

    send back, record minutes / costs / late credit, mark accepted, refund), Experts (create, switch on/off, agreement signed, reset
    two-factor / password, available / on-hold / paid, record a manual payout), Summary (the pilot numbers).
Expert accounts and workbench (the minimum; see DECISIONS 1)
  * Hand-created accounts; password plus authenticator code; its own token secret; no self-registration.
  * Workbench: structured editor; locked contact fields, third-party details and employer / dates / school anchors are enforced
    SERVER-SIDE (the browser is not the lock); up to three questions; submit for the operator's review.
  * An expert reads or writes a client's content only through database functions (human_job_expert_read / _save / _baseline) that
    refuse, and log the attempt, unless the job is assigned to that expert and in a working state.
Pilot recording
  * On the job row: active edit seconds (counted while typing, idle excluded), operator minutes override, questions, revision,
    paid / delivered times, score before and after, rating, other cost, late credit; job_events for every transition.
Client emails
  * Order received, expert question, delivered (new templates and senders, registered in the repo's email registry test).
    A refund uses the existing payment_reversed email.
Operator review and delivery (Appendix F.3)
  * Expert submits to in_review; the operator reads the restored resume, sees the re-score and any figure that is in nothing the
    customer gave us, then delivers (Word + PDF built by the app) or sends back with a note.
One revision, the 72-hour rule, earnings
  * One revision round (24-hour tier: 24 h clock, others 48 h, within 7 days, same expert). Silence for 72 hours is accepted by the
    hourly tick. Earnings are written ONCE on acceptance (a unique index is the last guard), held 3 days, reversed once on refund.
Retention, deletion, audit
  * Job files, working documents, locked values and supporting files are deleted after the retention period (90 days after the job
    ends), at once for a deleted account's finished jobs, and for unpaid orders after 24 hours. Every expert or staff view of client
    content and every denied attempt is written to expert_audit_log.

NOT BUILT (as instructed): claim queue, offer cascade, strikes, grades, graders, payout automation, applications and the paid test
flow, disputes console, the assistant, public explainer pages, marketplace settings UI.

5. DECISIONS AND DIFFERENCES (documents vs the code, and where I chose)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. Expert auth. Engineering spec puts two-factor columns, expert_sessions and the realm in Phase 2. The pilot has the MINIMUM:
    two-factor on expert_accounts, stateless tokens revoked by a token_version, NO expert_sessions table, NO recovery codes (the
    operator resets two-factor by hand), no idle timeout. Expert pages are a lazy chunk of the existing frontend with their own API
    client and sessionStorage key (spec 2.3 splits them into a separate site in Phase 2). FLAGGED, as your brief asked.
 2. "Settings table": the app has none. Settings are one row of system_state (key human_pilot), as the promo config does.
 3. payments.scan_id is NULL for human payments; the job holds the scan link (the existing checkout, orphan sweep and fix claim all
    key on a payment's scan, and one pending payment per scan is enforced by payments_scan_id_pending_uidx).
 4. Engineering spec Phase 1 lists payments.human_job_id; not added (human_jobs.payment_id UNIQUE is the link, and a two-way FK adds
    nothing). job_documents is unique on (job_id, kind, version), not (job_id, version), because source / draft / working coexist.
 5. Checkout wording is 06-checkout-wording as written. Its two "to confirm" lines are shown as written: "the expert may be in a
    different country" and "we credit you the rush premium automatically" (NOT automated in Phase 1; see gaps).
 6. Polish has no turnaround in either spec and the runbook says not to sell it until you set one: built, switched OFF in settings.
 7. The 24-hour tier is on, as you chose. Pilot cap is 15 as you gave; the Phase 1 gate needs 20 or more paid orders, so a cap of 15
    cannot meet the gate. It is a setting; raise it if you meant the gate to be reachable.
 8. Human orders are sold only while the active payment currency is USD (the settings are in dollars).
 9. A revision goes back through the operator's review (revision -> in_review), a transition the engineering spec's table does not list.
10. A refund uses the existing payment_reversed email; only three templates were added.
11. Materials: links are kept for the operator only (nothing here can fetch and mask a web page); files must be Word or text PDF.
12. human_job_expert_save stores a new document row per save (kept until retention); saves are compare-and-set on the version.

7. KNOWN GAPS (not audited by me; this is what I know is missing or unverified)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 * NOT VERIFIED FROM HERE: a live Supabase database (the migration and functions ran on PGlite, not Supabase), a real browser, real
   Paystack, a deployment, email delivery, Cloudflare Browser Rendering (PDF), R2, the Durable Object rate limiter, Microsoft Word.
 * A card dispute does not move the job to disputed (the webhook controller is untouched). The inbox shows the payment status.
 * The pilot-cap check is not atomic: two simultaneous checkouts on the last place could both be accepted. Paid orders are never refused.
 * The rush-premium credit and the full-refund-if-48-hours-late are NOT automated: "Late credit given" only records what you did by hand.
 * No two-factor recovery codes, no expert sessions table, no idle timeout, no expert accept / decline step in the app (you ask them).
 * Customers can cancel in the app only until an expert is assigned; afterwards they contact support.
 * Links in supporting material are not shown to experts. Photos / scans are refused as supporting files.
 * The company name is hidden from the job posting only when the existing extractor is sure of it.
 * Settings change by SQL; there is no admin screen. No cap on a customer starting many unpaid orders beyond the per-minute limit.
 * The addendum named in the checkout wording ("Human Rewrite Addendum") and the Terms / Privacy changes from Phase 0 do not exist in
   the app (public pages were out of scope). They must be live before the first paid order.
 * Account deletion is a soft delete in this app; finished jobs of a deleted account are purged by the sweep, open ones when they end.
 * Admin "force fulfil" (payments.controller.js line ~894, fulfillPayment with force) is not routed through the human path; use Recheck.
 * Frontend tests run in jsdom: no real browser, no layout, no Paystack popup.
 * The three spike resumes are synthetic (see section 2).
