Scan / ATS - round 14 (fresh independent pass over the whole section, then fixes)

FIXED
1. AI outage gave a permanent, lower, unflagged score (scan.controller.js, FixBanner.jsx, ScanResult.jsx, scan.routes.js)
   A scan whose AI half could not run was saved rule-only and held under the credential bar (rule 84 -> 79 instead of 86); the
   stored aiScored:false was read by nothing, the Badge was blocked, the pass/fail email carried the lower number, and the only
   way out was another daily scan.
   - buildAtsDetail now exposes scoredWithoutAi; ScanResult shows a "This score is provisional" notice with "Re-check my score".
   - POST /api/scan/:id/rescore-ai re-runs the same scoring on the same input at no quota cost, only for such a scan (owner or
     anon token, not purchased, no open Credential checkout, status-guarded write). 503 if the AI is still down.
   - FixBanner no longer says "being rejected by ATS filters" / "N points short" over a provisional score.
   - A provisional score is not mailed as the result; the email is sent after the re-check (notifyScanResult, extracted).
