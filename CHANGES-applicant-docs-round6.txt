APPLICANT-FACING DOCUMENTS - round 6 (base: upstream 5e52184; findings of the seventh independent pass)
No migration, no new dependency.

Gaps closed
- G1  The Word files take the PDF's body-text colour too. The PDF sets body copy in each palette's `text` (a deliberate dark tint,
      9:1 or better against white); the DOCX body was plain black. generateAtsDocx / generateCoverLetterDocx accept `text` (6-digit hex,
      else Word's automatic colour) as the document-default run colour; headings, links and the muted dates keep their own colours.
      scan.controller.js lookFor() / loadCoverLetter pass {accent, serif, text} from the same design tokens the PDF is rendered from.
- G2  A cover letter just over one page is tightened instead of leaving a signature alone on page 2. Since the letter became
      editable (up to 4,000 characters) that is easy to reach. lib/docFit.js letterDensityFor (by word count, measured against real
      renders on A4: PDF letter holds ~405 words normal / ~465 compact, DOCX ~505 / ~600; set at the safe end): compact is a smaller
      type size, tighter leading and paragraph spacing (PDF) / 10.5pt, tighter spacing and margins (DOCX). A clearly longer letter is
      left alone. Verified: PDF letters up to 440 words and DOCX letters up to 560 words now fit one page (PDF spilled from 420,
      DOCX from ~510).
