partner pages and useReferralCapture (badge.service.js turned out to be the verification short-code generator and

The Worker's dashboard / list / detail queries select the new ledger columns and fail without the migration.

BUG FIXED
 B1  Removing a partner's personal data left their address behind in email_logs (every partner mail ever sent to
     it) and alert_logs (owner alerts quote the partner's name, email and payout-target tail) until the 90 / 180-day
     retention sweep, although the sibling erasure flows (deleteAccount, employer-lead removal) purge email_logs.
     adminAnonymizePartner now deletes the partner-template email_logs rows for the address (candidate mail is never
     touched) and the alert_logs rows that quote the address or the partner id. Best effort and last: a failed purge
     never fails the erasure, is reported (logsPurged) and falls back to the retention sweep. Skipped for a partner
     that was already removed. (partners.controller.js purgePartnerLogs)
     Not changed on purpose: an admin email CHANGE keeps the old address's logs, because the security notice sent to
     the old address is deliberately retained evidence.

SMALLER FIXES
 s1  Dashboard (and admin partner detail) ledger embeds ordered by created_at only, while /conversions orders by
     created_at then id: rows sharing a timestamp could be duplicated or missed at the 50 / 51 boundary. Same tiebreak.
 s2  createPartnerSchema / updatePartnerSchema had no email max length (applications cap at 320) and the update schema
     did not trim. Both now cap at 320; update trims.
 s3  adminListPartners summed raw cents across currencies and never flagged it (only adminGetPartner did). The list now
     carries mixedCurrency; such partners are left out of "Record payout run" / "Export payout run" and called out.
 s4  Approving an application ignored a failed partner_id back-link update. Erasure follows that link (the email
     fallback is defeated by an email change). Retried once and logged.
 s5  A taken referral code on the partner page surfaced as the generic 409 "Already exists."; now says so.
 s6  Dashboard label "N conversions" counted reversal rows as well; it now reads "N entries".

FEATURE GAPS CLOSED
 G1  Manual ledger adjustments (migration 0069, POST /api/partners/:id/adjustments). The ledger could only hold a sale
     and the reversal of one, so there was no way to claw back fraudulent commission, grant a goodwill bonus or write
     off an unpayable balance — and an unpayable balance also blocked erasing the partner (anonymize needs a zero
     balance, and a partner with no payout details cannot be paid). An adjustment is a commission_ledger row of kind
     ADJUSTMENT: signed non-zero amount (max 1,000,000.00), mandatory reason (3-500 chars), no payment, no code.
       - settles, voids and nets exactly like every other unpaid row (payout claim, void release, balance and
         anonymize checks all read commission_amount_cents);
       - treated as a credit row by the cycle logic: a cycle-scoped payout pulls unpaid adjustments from other closed
         cycles in with it, and the Pay button's default amount matches;
       - never held (not subject to the refund-hold window), never counted as a conversion;
       - the partner is emailed the amount and reason (admin can untick) and sees the row, with its reason, on the
         dashboard and in their CSV export; the audit log records amount and ledger id, not the reason text;
       - erasure scrubs the reason text (the amount stays) and refuses with a pointer to write-off when a balance
         remains. Admin UI: "Adjust balance…" (deduct / add, "fill in the amount that brings the balance to zero").
 G2  Terms acceptance and re-acceptance. Only applicants ever recorded terms_accepted_at; partners created by an admin
     never did, and a PARTNER_TERMS_VERSION bump had no path although the terms page promises an email first.
       - POST /api/partners/accept-terms (either partner token, idempotent) records the current version;
       - dashboard shows a prompt until accepted (not a gate on payouts — that is a policy call);
       - partner page shows accepted / older version / not accepted; list shows who is outstanding;
       - POST /api/partners/terms-notice (admin) emails ACTIVE partners who have not accepted the current version,
         40 per run, once per partner per version (partners.terms_notified_version), resumable.
 G3  Admin can edit website / audience and keep admin-only internal notes (partners.internal_notes). '' clears.
     Notes are never selected by any token-gated endpoint and are cleared at erasure. Audit logs field names only.
 G4  Partner-side transparency: the dashboard shows the account payouts go to (method, bank/provider, last four only)
     and any hold still running after a payout-details change; the payout-details save screen says that a change holds
     the next payout and until when; the refund-credit banner now reads "refunds or balance adjustments".

MIGRATION 0069 (idempotent)
 commission_ledger: payment_id and referral_code_id become nullable; kind ('SALE' default | 'ADJUSTMENT'),
 adjustment_reason, created_by; CHECK keeps every SALE row (original or reversal) tied to a payment and a code and
 every ADJUSTMENT row payment-less, code-less, non-reversing, reasoned and non-zero. partners: internal_notes,
 terms_notified_version. Verified against Postgres (PGlite): applies twice cleanly, existing rows stay SALE, valid
 clawbacks/bonuses insert, all malformed shapes are rejected.
