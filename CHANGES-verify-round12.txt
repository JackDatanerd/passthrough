BUGS
B1 (medium) admin.controller.js adminListVerificationReports: the queue sorted `ascending: status !== 'OPEN'`, i.e. OPEN
   reports NEWEST first and closed ones oldest first — the reverse of round 10's intent and of the panel's own
   "Showing the oldest N" line. With more than one page of open reports the oldest ones were the ones starved. Now OPEN is
   oldest first, closed newest first, with `id` as a tie-break so equal timestamps cannot swap places between pages.
   Tests: the two round-8/round-10 assertions only matched the buggy SOURCE TEXT (the shared in-memory double does not
   sort), which is why this survived; they are replaced by verify.round12.test.js, which runs against a stub that really
   sorts and fails (3/3) against the old expression.
B2 (low-medium) lib/text.js firstNameOf: "Surname, First" was only recognised when the comma sat in the FIRST word, so
   "Dr. Doe, John" printed "Doe" and "van der Berg, Jan" / "De La Cruz, Maria" printed "van" / "De" under
   "Passthrough Verified", in the link-preview title and on the dashboard. A comma after up to three name particles + one
   word is now read as surname-first (after any leading title); a credential/suffix after the comma ("John Smith, PhD",
   "Kimani, Jr.", "Njeri Kamau, CPA(K)") or a job title ("Jane Doe, Senior Engineer") keeps the name as written. "John, PhD"
   used to hide the name entirely and now reads "John". Also: "Mba" is a real surname/given name (Igbo, Gabonese) and no
   longer counts as a credential unless written in capitals ("MBA Grace" still skips the title).
   The wrong token was STORED at write time (candidate_first_name), so fixing the parser cannot repair existing pages:
   scripts/backfill-candidate-names.cjs re-derives it from the resume data (user-edited, else rewritten, else original).
