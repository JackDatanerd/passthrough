RESUME FROM SCRATCH — round 2 (independent re-audit: G1, B1–B4)

BUGS
B1  A valid hand-filled resume could hard-fail. runAtsScan's 100-character floor (meant for empty FILES) rejected anything that
    renders shorter: one job + one bullet + name + email is ~76 characters, a skills-only form ~47. The scan ended TOO_SHORT -
    "we found almost no text in this file, upload a new one" - non-retryable, no editor (only shown for completed scans), and
    the form was gone. Structured scans (typed form, typed background, saved profile) that carry real content are now scored as
    they are (honestly low when thin; the editor and "Worth adding" hints are right there). One with nothing in it fails as
    NEEDS_MORE_DETAIL instead of TOO_SHORT. Uploaded files keep TOO_SHORT.
B2  ScanResult cleared the saved draft whenever ANY from-scratch result was open, so opening an old result (history, e-mail, other
    tab) deleted a new background that was half typed. ScanForm now notes which scan it sent and what that part of the draft held
    (lib/brainDumpDraft.js markDraftSent); only that scan's result clears it (clearDraftIfSent), and only if nothing different was
    typed since. Sending the typed text leaves a half-filled form alone and the reverse.
B3  raw_brain_dump_text stayed on a scan that ended ERROR for the life of the account (the "wiped once a result exists" comment only
    held for success). A failure retrying cannot fix (NEEDS_MORE_DETAIL / thin structured input) now drops it at once; retryable
    failures keep it for retry-scan; retention.service.js purgeFailedBackgrounds clears any scan left ERROR for 7 days (no migration).
B4  Smaller:
    - A signed-in person who left name/email blank on the hand-filled form had their ACCOUNT name/email printed on the resume
      ("nothing is guessed" on the form). The fallback now only applies to data the model just structured from free text; the form
      starts with the account name/email visible and editable, and the editor hints at a missing name.
    - manualHasContent refused awards/publications-only forms the server accepts (parity with hasResumeContent).
    - Hand-filled scans are stored as brain_dump, so they showed "couldn't structure your background" / "built from your text".
      Copy is neutral now; the failure "start over" link opens the form when that is what was kept.
    - GET /scan/:id/download-draft-pdf shared the PAID regenerate-pdf bucket (rl.pdfRegen). Own rl.draftPdf bucket, same size.

GAPS
G1  The "Fill it in myself" form was never kept (the typed background was, since round 1). It is saved in the same draft as you go,
    comes back after a reload / failed scan with a notice, and the form tab reopens on it (an explicit ?mode= link still wins).
(Upstream's shared lib/links.js already makes the PDF contact line clickable, so the second gap from this pass is closed there.)
