B2  Multipart CRLF counted double against the length cap (text silently truncated). upload.js normalises to LF first.
B3  Anonymous visitor kept their 1/hour slot when the background job failed. Slot is now refunded (job, waitUntil fallback, dead-letter).
B4  Magic-link ?token was not persisted; revisiting lost access. ScanResult now stores it.
B5  CsvInput kept stale text after removing a row (wrong technologies shown). Now re-syncs from props.
B6  Saving an empty resume in the editor was accepted. updateResumeData now 400s.
B7  Anonymous name/email typed in the form now fill what structuring leaves empty.
B8  <background> tag was not stripped by stripPromptTags (prompt-injection surface).
B9  raw_brain_dump_text kept forever. Nulled on completion; interrupted reruns reuse structured data.
B10 Rescan of a from-scratch scan was relabelled saved_profile. Stays brain_dump.
