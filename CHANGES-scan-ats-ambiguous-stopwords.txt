SCAN/ATS - ambiguous foreign stop words (bug)

BUG
- src/services/ats.service.js stop-listed Spanish/French/Portuguese/German/Italian/Swahili function words unconditionally. Several are
  also English words or tech terms: die, pour, nas, dos, los, las, com, con, sin, son, den, fur, est, des, ces, ses, uno, para, mas, ser,
  sera, dal, als, pia, nos, tra. In an English posting they were dropped, so "Die Casting Operator" never asked for "die", a sysadmin
  posting never asked for NAS or DOS, a concrete-pour or barista posting never asked for "pour", and a resume with or without them
  scored identically on keywords. The file's own comment says such words are deliberately not in the list.

FIX
- Those words moved to AMBIGUOUS_FOREIGN_STOP. They are stop words only when the posting itself is not English (postingIsForeign on the
  posting text), which is the only place they are function words. extractKeywords and extractWeightedKeywords take the test per posting.
- NON_ENGLISH_STOP is unchanged, so looksEnglish (which counts foreign function words) behaves exactly as before.

NOTE
- "Not English" is decided by postingIsForeign: at least 3 unambiguous foreign function words and 3% of the text. looksEnglish was tried
  first and rejected: it calls a posting under 60 words, or one diluted by English tech terms, English (tests/scan.ats.round9 caught it).

TESTS
- tests/ats.ambiguousStopWords.test.js (4): English/tech terms survive in both extractors; a die casting posting asks for "die casting";
  NAS changes the keyword score; a French posting still filters "pour". Three of the four fail without the fix.
