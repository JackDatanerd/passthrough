No new env var.

GAPS
G1  GET /api/employer-leads/:id (admin): the whole lead, its do-not-contact state and reason, the global mail block, the
    employer mail sent to the address, and OTHER leads for the same inbox. Admin list has a "Details" button for it.
G2  The admin list flags rows on the do-not-contact list ("do not contact"), from one batched lookup per page.
G3  "Update my details" link in the acknowledgement and candidates emails: POST /employer-leads/preferences/load and
    /preferences (new signed-token purpose "prefs", rate-limited like the other public links) and a public page
    /employer/preferences. Edits main field, up to 4 other fields and job title only - never name, company or status.

BUGS
B1  A removal ("remove me", complaint, bounce, admin do-not-contact, delete-and-block, bulk delete-and-block) deleted only
    the typed and canonical spellings, so a lead stored as dana+jobs@gmail.com survived dana@gmail.com's removal - with its
    name and company - despite the promise on the remove page. employer_leads.mailbox_key (migration 0076, kept by a
    trigger, backfilled) holds the inbox; every removal path now deletes by exact address AND mailbox_key in two statements
    per 50 inboxes, purges the logs of every removed spelling, and reports/audits "alsoRemoved". Works before 0076.
B2  The hourly acknowledgement sweep only SKIPPED a suppressed lead, never marking it, so enough of them sat at the head of
    the queue every hour and starved the leads behind. It now looks the batch up in one query, deletes leads whose address
    asked to be removed, and closes out other suppressed leads.
B3  notify-candidates examined one fixed window of 100 leads; unmailable leads that are never stamped filled it and nobody
    behind them was reached. The window now moves (keyset on created_at, id; up to 500 examined), the list is checked a page
    at a time, removal-request leads are deleted ("removed" in the response), dry-run eligibility excludes blocked leads.
B4  Single "send confirm link" mailed a do-not-contact address; now 409 DO_NOT_CONTACT. Bulk request-confirmation counts
    them in "doNotContact".
B5  A filter-wide bulk action that failed part-way discarded its audit entry and answered a bare 500 although some leads had
    changed. It now audits what changed (partial: true) and answers 500 code PARTIAL with how many.
B6  Editing a lead's email recorded no consent for the new address (the old consent stayed); it now records
    kind admin_edit with the replaced kind and the admin.
B7  A failed do-not-contact lift during an edit (or a re-join) left a lead that is never mailed and no way to retry: edit now
    answers 409 REMOVAL_LIFT_FAILED (saved: true; the dialog offers a retry that carries overrideRemoval), and the re-join
    retry lifts a self-placed block when the lead already exists.
B8  CSV-imported leads were stored as source 'manual'; now 'import' (existing rows relabelled by 0076; filter/labels added).
B9  Three comments pointed at migration 0064 for candidates_notified_fields; it is 0068.
